## Japan, Kyoto
### 2025/02/08

It's not the first snowfall of the day.
In fact, there was snow here and there a few times in January and December.
However, it's the first heavy snow day in Kyoto this year. Today brings back the memory of the blizzard that visited Kyoto a few years ago.
